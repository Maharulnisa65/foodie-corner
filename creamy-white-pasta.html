Creamy White Pasta Recipe

When you want something comforting and fast, creamy white pasta is hard to beat. The sauce is made in one pan with butter, garlic, milk and cheese, so there is very little to wash up.

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves2

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 1 cup milk
  • 50 g cheese, grated
  • 1 tbsp garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp flour (optional, for a thicker sauce)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until just tender, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan and fry the garlic for 30 seconds without browning it.
  3. Stir in the flour if using, then slowly add the milk and cook until it begins to thicken.
  4. Add the cheese, salt and pepper and stir until the sauce is smooth.
  5. Add the pasta, mix well and serve hot.

Tips for the best creamy white pasta

  • Save a small cup of pasta water and use it to loosen a sauce that gets too thick.
  • Add boiled chicken, mushrooms or sweetcorn for a heavier meal.
  • Grate the cheese yourself, it melts more smoothly than pre-grated cheese.
