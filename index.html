Chicken Biryani - 45 min Ajza: Chawal 2 cup, Chicken adha kilo, Dahi adha cup, Biryani masala 2 chamach, Pyaz 2, Tamatar 2, Hari mirch, Dhania Tareeqa: 1. Chawal 30 min bhigo dein. 2. Pyaz brown kar ke chicken, dahi, masala dal kar bhoon lein. 3. Chawal alag ubal lein. 4. Degchi me teh lagayen - chawal, salan, dhania. 5. 15 min dum par rakhen.
Creamy White Pasta - 20 min Ajza: Pasta 200 gram, Doodh 1 cup, Cheese 50 gram, Lehsan 1 chamach, Makhan 1 chamach, Namak, Kali mirch Tareeqa: 1. Pasta ubal lein. 2. Makhan me lehsan bhonen, doodh aur cheese dal kar sauce banayen. 3. Pasta dal kar mix karen.
Fresh Garden Salad - 10 min Ajza: Salad patta, Kheera 1, Tamatar 1, Gajar 1, Lemon 1, Zaitoon ka tail 1 chamach, Namak Tareeqa: 1. Sab sabziyan bareek kaat lein. 2. Lemon ka ras aur tail dal kar mix karen. 3. Thanda kar ke pesh karen.
Chocolate Cake - 60 min Ajza: Maida 1 cup, Cheeni adha cup, Cocoa powder 3 chamach, 2 anday, Doodh adha cup, Makhan 50 gram Tareeqa: 1. Anday aur cheeni achi tarah phent lein. 2. Maida, cocoa, doodh, makhan dal kar batter banayen. 3. 180 degree par 40 min bake karen.
