Chicken Biryani - 45 min Ingredients: 2 cups Rice, 500g Chicken, 1/2 cup Yogurt, 2 tbsp Biryani Masala, 2 Onions, 2 Tomatoes, Green Chilies, Fresh Coriander Method: 1. Soak rice for 30 minutes. 2. Fry onions, add chicken, yogurt and masala and cook. 3. Boil rice separately. 4. Make layers in pot - rice, chicken curry, coriander. 5. Keep on dum for 15 minutes.
Creamy White Pasta - 20 min Ingredients: 200g Pasta, 1 cup Milk, 50g Cheese, 1 tbsp Garlic, 1 tbsp Butter, Salt, Black Pepper Method: 1. Boil pasta. 2. Fry garlic in butter, add milk and cheese to make white sauce. 3. Add pasta and mix well. Serve hot.
Fresh Garden Salad - 10 min Ingredients: Lettuce, 1 Cucumber, 1 Tomato, 1 Carrot, 1 Lemon, 1 tbsp Olive Oil, Salt Method: 1. Chop all vegetables. 2. Add lemon juice and olive oil and mix. 3. Serve chilled.
Chocolate Cake - 60 min Ingredients: 1 cup Flour, 1/2 cup Sugar, 3 tbsp Cocoa Powder, 2 Eggs, 1/2 cup Milk, 50g Butter Method: 1. Beat eggs and sugar well. 2. Add flour, cocoa, milk and butter to make batter. 3. Bake at 180 degrees for 40 minutes.
