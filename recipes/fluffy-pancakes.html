Fluffy Pancakes Recipe

Fluffy pancakes are one of the easiest breakfasts to make from scratch. The batter comes together in one bowl and the secret to a soft, tall pancake is not over-mixing it.

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves3
🥞

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • A pinch of salt
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp melted butter, plus extra for the pan

Method

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
  2. In another bowl whisk the egg, milk and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined. A few small lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan on medium heat and grease it lightly with butter.
  5. Pour a small ladle of batter, cook until bubbles appear on top, then flip and cook for another minute.
  6. Serve warm with honey, fruit or maple syrup.

Tips for the best fluffy pancakes

  • Rest the batter for 5 minutes for a softer pancake.
  • Keep the heat at medium so the pancakes brown evenly without burning.
  • Do not press the pancake with the spatula after flipping.
