Chocolate Cake Recipe

This is a simple everyday chocolate cake made with ingredients you probably already have. It is soft, rich and perfect for birthdays or a quiet afternoon with tea.

  • Prep20 min
  • Cook40 min
  • Total60 min
  • Serves8

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 50 g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a medium cake tin.
  2. Beat the eggs and sugar well until pale and fluffy.
  3. Sift in the flour, cocoa and baking powder, then add the milk, melted butter and vanilla. Mix gently into a smooth batter.
  4. Pour the batter into the tin and bake for about 40 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  5. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.

Tips for the best chocolate cake

  • Do not open the oven door in the first 25 minutes or the cake may sink.
  • Sift the cocoa to avoid lumps.
  • Cool the cake completely before adding any frosting.
