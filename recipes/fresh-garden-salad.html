Fresh Garden Salad Recipe

A good salad is all about fresh, crisp vegetables and a light dressing. This one takes ten minutes, needs no cooking and goes well with biryani, grilled chicken or pasta.

  • Prep10 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves2

Ingredients

  • 1 small head of lettuce
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Wash all the vegetables well and pat them dry.
  2. Chop the lettuce, cucumber, tomato and carrot into bite-size pieces.
  3. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oil and salt together in a small bowl.
  4. Pour the dressing over the vegetables, mix and serve chilled.

Tips for the best fresh garden salad

  • Add the dressing just before serving so the vegetables stay crisp.
  • Chill the vegetables for 15 minutes before you mix the salad.
  • Try a pinch of black pepper or a few mint leaves for extra flavour.
