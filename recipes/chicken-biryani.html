Chicken Biryani Recipe

Chicken biryani is the dish that makes any dinner feel like a celebration. This version keeps the steps simple: a quick yogurt and masala chicken, par-boiled rice, and a short dum to bring the flavours together.

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook30 min
  • Total45 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3-4 green chilies
  • A handful of fresh coriander
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Wash the rice and soak it in water for 30 minutes while you prepare the chicken.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add the chicken, yogurt, tomatoes, biryani masala, green chilies and salt. Cook until the chicken is tender and the gravy thickens.
  4. Boil the soaked rice separately in salted water until it is about 70% cooked, then drain.
  5. Layer the rice over the chicken curry and sprinkle with fresh coriander.
  6. Cover tightly and keep on dum (low heat) for 15 minutes, then gently mix and serve.

Tips for the best chicken biryani

  • Use aged basmati rice for long, separate grains.
  • Do not cook the rice fully before layering, it finishes cooking in the dum.
  • Place a folded kitchen towel under the lid so steam does not escape.
