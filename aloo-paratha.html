Aloo Paratha Recipe

Aloo paratha is a classic breakfast: soft whole wheat dough stuffed with spiced potato and cooked until golden. Serve it hot with yogurt, pickle or a cup of chai.

  • Prep25 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total40 min
  • Serves4
🫓

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta)
  • 3 boiled potatoes, mashed
  • 1 tsp roasted cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1-2 green chilies, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus a pinch for the dough
  • Ghee or oil for cooking
  • Water, as needed for the dough

Method

  1. Mix the flour with a pinch of salt and add water gradually to make a soft dough. Rest it for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the mashed potatoes with cumin, chili powder, green chilies, coriander and salt.
  3. Divide the dough into balls. Flatten one ball, place a spoonful of filling in the centre and seal the edges.
  4. Roll it gently into a round paratha, dusting with flour as needed.
  5. Cook on a hot tawa, brushing ghee on both sides, until golden brown spots appear.

Tips for the best aloo paratha

  • Let the boiled potatoes cool and dry out so the filling is not sticky.
  • Roll gently and evenly so the filling does not tear through.
  • Cook on medium heat so the paratha cooks inside and turns crisp outside.
