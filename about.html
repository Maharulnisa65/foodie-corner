About Foodie Corner

Welcome to Foodie Corner! My name is Maharulnisa and I started this blog to share easy and tasty recipes for everyday life.

What we do

Our goal is to help busy people, students and new cooks make delicious food at home with simple ingredients. Every recipe lists exact quantities, gives clear steps and ends with a few tips.

How recipes are written

All recipes are tested at home and written in simple language. We try to use ingredients that are easy to find in an ordinary kitchen, and we list the time and number of servings so you can plan your meal.

Fresh recipes every week

We post new recipes every week. You can browse all recipes, or send us a recipe request and we will try to cook it for you.

Thank you for visiting Foodie Corner.
